    HOW IT WORKS

    Two roulette wheels (0–36) each lock on a hidden number. Final score = Wheel 1 + Wheel 2 (0–72). The target is 12–60 (middle targets are rare). Bet HIGHER (score above the target), LOWER (below) or CENTER (exactly the target — a long shot with a big payout). If the score hits the target, HIGHER and LOWER both lose.

    The reveal

    Each wheel narrows down in 5 steps. Every step knocks out a whole sector (a run of 5, 6, 7, 8 and finally 10 neighbouring pockets), so you are predicting where the ball will land. One pocket is left at the end. Wheel 1 resolves first, then Wheel 2. Odds, win chance and the wheel colours update after every step.

    Your decisions
    • Hold – keep your position, reveal the next step.
    • Boost – buy more potential win on your side at today's odds (doubles your potential payout).
    • Flip – sell your position, buy the opposite side for the same potential payout.
    • Cash out – take the cash value now and end the round. The real numbers are still revealed afterwards.
    Lucky pockets

    Each round 1–2 pockets are marked gold with a multiplier (×2 to ×10). If the ball ends on one and your side wins, your win is multiplied. To pay for those big wins, an ordinary win pays less: the more likely a lucky win is for your side, the lower the number on its button. So a very likely side can work out at less than x1.00. Such a side can't be bet: its button turns into 🔀 NEW TARGET, which swaps the target for another random one (the hidden results and lucky pockets stay the same). Over many rounds it evens out to the same 98% as every other bet.

    Doubles: money back

    If your bet loses but both wheels end on the same number (7 and 7, 22 and 22…), you get your stake back: no win, no loss.

    Your lucky number: craps bonus

    Pick your own lucky number (🎲 button next to your balance, 36 by default; a birthday works). If your bet loses and either wheel ends on your number, you play a round of craps: 7 or 11 wins, 2, 3 or 12 loses, anything else becomes your point and you roll until the point (win) or a 7 (lose). Win it and you get your lost stake back. It works about half the time, and it is priced into the odds, so every number is equally fair. Doubles count first: double 36s give your stake back without a roll.

    Auto-hold

    When every remaining pair already wins for one side the outcome is decided, so the rest plays out automatically.

    Fairness

    Odds = (98% × possible pairs − 244/495 × losing pairs that hold your lucky number − losing doubles) ÷ winning pairs, each winning pair counted with its lucky multiplier (RTP 98%), rounded down. Position value = Σ stake × (odds × win chance + 244/495 × craps chance + doubles chance). Cash-out = value × 0.95, capped at 95% of the stake. Rounding always favours the house.

    Wheel colours

    The glowing ring marks each number: ▲ green = good for your side (over half of the other wheel's numbers win with it), ▼ red = bad, – grey = even.
